How to play

Put your finger on the circle at the bottom and push up, down, left or right: the plane turns that way (keyboard: arrows or A, D). It never stops moving.

The sky moves from morning to night as your plane evolves.

Stars appear one at a time. Every 5 stars your plane evolves through 30 unique aircraft. A missile hit drops you 2 levels. A hit at Level 1 ends the run.

From Level 4 your plane shoots by itself, and later planes get stronger guns and homing missiles. The first three planes have no guns, so dodge.

Bosses arrive at Levels 9, 17 and 25, and a Final Challenge at Level 30. Survive until the red bar empties, or shoot down the big boss missile.

Pass close to a missile for near-miss bonus points. Combos stack.

Collect ◈ coins from stars, destroyed missiles and bosses. Spend them in the Hangar Upgrade Bay. Every five levels unlocks a checkpoint rescue option after a crash.

Tap the blue ability button beside Pause, or press Space, for your current aircraft family ability: shield, magnet, slow time, overdrive, afterburner or nova wave.

Watch for world hazards: turbulence, mines, laser gates, drones and asteroids. Each sky world brings a new threat.
