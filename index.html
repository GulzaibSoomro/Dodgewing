How to play

Flight controls

Turn with the joystick or drag in Touch Anywhere. Steer around missiles and hazards. Tap Pause to stop the flight, then Resume when ready.

Collect stars

Fly into the single mission star. Another appears in a random position after two seconds of active flight. Your wallet keeps every earned star, even after a crash.

Fuel survival

A new aircraft has 60 seconds of fuel. Fuel drains while flying and an empty tank ends the attempt. Each retry refills the tank for free.

Aircraft Forge

Spend wallet stars on a larger tank or better equipment. Each aircraft has its own upgrades.

Campaign progression

Each of the 30 levels assigns one aircraft. The final star saves a checkpoint and refills your tank as the boss arrives. Destroy the boss or survive its timer to unlock the next level.

Temporary power-ups

Shield absorbs a hit. Magnet pulls the current star toward you. Slow Motion slows missiles while your fuel and star timers keep running.
